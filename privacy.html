Syrine Job Application Assistant

Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Syrine Job Application Assistant is a personal job-application tool operated by Syrine Dhifellaoui. It is intended for the operator's own use and is not a public recruitment service. Contact: syrine.dhifellaoui9@gmail.com.

Information accessed

After the account owner authorizes Google access, the tool reads designated CV and cover-letter references through Google Docs and Drive. It handles document text and formatting, file identifiers, titles and revision information, and generated document copies and exports. Reference content can include the candidate's name, contact details, education, qualifications, employment history and achievements.

The workflow also processes vacancy descriptions, candidate-provided facts, application answers and supporting documents, application status and confirmation evidence, and outcomes or follow-up information entered by the candidate. OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored by the local n8n installation to maintain the authorized Google connection; the tool does not request the Google account password.

Purposes of processing

Information is used to assess job suitability, prepare tailored German applications, check factual accuracy and document quality, export PDFs and editable versions, support authorized application steps, avoid duplicates, and maintain application history and follow-ups. Google document copies are edited for these purposes while the designated master documents serve as references.

Services that receive information

  • Google: stores reference documents and generated copies and processes document-reading, copying, editing and export requests.
  • OpenAI API: receives selected reference-document content, candidate facts, vacancy information and draft text for job matching, tailoring and quality review. AI processing is an external service; it does not take place entirely on the operator's computer.
  • Apify: processes configured job-search requests and public vacancy information for discovery.
  • Employers and application platforms: receive candidate information, documents and answers when the candidate authorizes application activity. Uploading documents or filling an online form may transmit information before the final submission button is clicked.

The operator does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. The tool uses Google data to provide the application features described here. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs must adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Google-hosted references and generated copies remain in the authorized Google account until deleted there. Exported documents, application records and supporting evidence are stored by the local worker. The n8n installation may retain inputs, outputs and errors in execution history. Copies may also exist in backups or folders synchronized by the operator.

The operator retains application material while needed for application preparation, tracking and follow-up, and manages deletion from local storage, workflow history, cloud documents and backups. Revoking OAuth access does not automatically delete information already stored. External providers apply their own service terms and retention settings to information processed by them.

Control, revocation and deletion

The account owner can stop the workflow and remove its Google credential in n8n. Google access can also be revoked through Google Account connections. This prevents further access through that authorization but does not erase existing local or cloud copies.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information held by the operator, contact syrine.dhifellaoui9@gmail.com. Information already sent to an employer or another provider is subject to that recipient's own handling and deletion procedures.

This public website

These informational pages do not contain an application form, analytics scripts or advertising trackers and do not access Google account data. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the tool's data handling changes. Material changes to Google-data use require an updated disclosure and any necessary consent before the new use begins.
