Personal application preparation

Syrine Job Application Assistant

A personal automation tool operated by Syrine Dhifellaoui to evaluate vacancies and prepare individually tailored German CVs and cover letters.

What the assistant does

The assistant compares job requirements with verified experience, selects relevant evidence, and prepares application documents. It checks the content and exported PDFs before they are used in an application.

Why it connects to Google

With the account owner's authorization, the assistant reads designated CV and cover-letter references in Google Docs, creates copies in Google Drive, edits those copies for a vacancy, and exports editable DOCX files and final PDFs. The master documents remain the references for future applications.

How information is processed

The workflow runs in a locally hosted n8n installation. Selected reference-document content, candidate facts and vacancy information are sent to OpenAI's API for matching, drafting and quality review. Documents are stored in Google Drive and local application storage; workflow execution records may also contain processed information.

This website provides information about the personal tool. It does not provide public account registration or collect application documents. Read the privacy policy for details about data handling and revoking Google access.
